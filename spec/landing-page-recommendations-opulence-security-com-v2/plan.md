## New section: "Your venue, always in the loop" (the bespoke system)
Three pillars that mirror the user's own words. Each has a one-line benefit, then 3 proof points:

1. **Up to date: you and us, in real time**
   - A venue portal shows who is on your door right now, with name, photo and SIA badge.
   - Incident reports reach you the same night, with photos, timeline and witness details.
   - Request extra cover or a new event from your phone, and get confirmation and staff names.
2. **More efficient: less admin, fewer surprises**
   - Hours are proven by NFC/GPS clock-in. You sign off timesheets in one tap, and invoices match exactly.
   - Our team rota, cover gaps and officer documents run in one system, so cover isn't lost to WhatsApp chains.
   - Contracts and assignment instructions are signed electronically, so every officer arrives briefed on your venue.
3. **Compliant and secure: ready for your licensing board**
   - Every officer's SIA licence and right to work is checked, with expiry alerts before anything lapses.
   - Shift logs, refusals and ban lists are kept as evidence for licensing reviews and the police.
   - Venue-specific procedures (site packs) are written, versioned and acknowledged by each officer.

Visual: a phone/laptop mock-up of the venue portal (live on-site view + an incident card) beside the pillars. Brand it Opulence. Keep the "Powered by orbsolutions.co.uk" line where the app shows it.

## "How it works" (4 steps)
1. Tell us your venue, nights and risks →
2. We build your site pack and brief vetted officers →
3. Officers clock in on site with proof, and you watch live →
4. Reports, signed timesheets and invoices land in your portal.
