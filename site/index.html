How we estimate this

Venue and staff numbers are live from our system. Hours and savings are estimates: a rough figure for the time venue teams spend checking records, retrieving reports and chasing updates, using these allowances:

  • 5 minutes for each weekly site timesheet a venue approves. Each site-week counts once, including any later amendments.
  • 5 minutes for each incident report a venue opens. Repeat views of the same report count once.
  • 2 minutes for each cover request answered through the portal.

To turn venues and staff into a year, we assume twelve four-week months, one incident report for every five staff each month, and one cover request per venue each week. Money saved values those hours at £15 an hour, a typical venue manager’s rate, plus 15% employer National Insurance.

Example for one venue: four weekly timesheet approvals, six incident reports retrieved and four cover enquiries resolved represent an estimated 58 minutes of management time saved.

Opulence’s own payroll checks, staff chasing and internal administration are not counted. These are illustrative estimates, not a measurement or a guarantee.
