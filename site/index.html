Our bespoke system

Your venue, always in the loop.

Every Opulence shift runs on our own platform. It keeps you and us up to date, takes the admin out of security, and keeps your venue compliant and secure.

i.

Up to date, you and us

See what’s happening on your door as it happens.

  • Your venue portal shows who is on your door right now, with name, photo and SIA badge.
  • Incident reports reach you the same night, with photos, timeline and witness details.
  • Request extra cover or a new event from your phone, and see who’s booked.
ii.

More efficient

Less admin and fewer surprises, for both of us.

  • Hours are proven by on-site clock-in. Sign off timesheets in one tap, and invoices match exactly.
  • Rota, cover gaps and officer documents run in one system, not in WhatsApp chains.
  • Contracts and assignment instructions are signed electronically, so every officer arrives briefed on your venue.
iii.

Compliant & secure

Ready for your licensing board, every night.

  • Every officer’s SIA licence and right to work is checked, with alerts before anything expires.
  • Shift logs, refusals and ban lists are kept as evidence for licensing reviews and the police.
  • Your venue’s procedures are written down, kept up to date and acknowledged by every officer.

How we estimate this

The number of venues is live from our system. Staff is our current team of about 35 officers until everyone is onboarded, then the live count. Hours and savings are estimates, using these planning figures:

  • A typical venue runs 3 shifts a week with 3 officers on each, at around 5 hours per officer. Across our venues that is about 216 officer shifts and 1,080 hours of cover every week.
  • Each officer saves the venue about 10 minutes of admin a week: incident reports, timesheets, finding records and compliance files.
  • Each shift saves about 5 minutes of chasing a security manager who may be in a meeting or at another site.

We add those up over 52 weeks, and value the time at a typical venue admin wage plus employer costs.

Opulence’s own payroll checks, staff chasing and internal administration are not counted. These are illustrative estimates, not a measurement or a guarantee.
